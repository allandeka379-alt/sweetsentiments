— Studio note

You choose the moment. We make the memory.

Sweet Sentiments is a curated gifting house. We make hampers, money bouquets, chocolate towers, balloon arrangements and full-room romantic surprise setups — for the people who want their gestures to be felt, delivered wherever the moment lives.

The studio started in 2024 with a single brief — a friend's apology bouquet — that took an entire afternoon to fold by hand. We delivered it that evening; the apology was accepted; we got two more orders by the end of the week.

We've stayed small on purpose. Every gift is hand-assembled at the studio, photographed before it leaves us, and dispatched the same day. We hand-fold every dollar bill in our money bouquets. We hand-write every card. The brand promise — you choose the moment, we make the memory — is also the studio's working method.

The atelier.

A converted front room with cream walls, a long folding-table, and a wall of grosgrain ribbon spools. We work to natural light. We photograph against the cream wall or the small garden out back. The studio is open by appointment for collections; most clients prefer we deliver.

The founders.

Rutendo and Anotidaishe founded Sweet Sentiments and run every part of it. They source the chocolate, fold the dollar bills, tie the ribbons, take the photographs, and hand over the gifts. There is no team behind the team — every sentiment that leaves the studio has been touched by one of them.

How we work.

  • Every gift is photographed before it leaves us. The photo is sent to the buyer.
  • Every order includes a free hand-written card. We will write what you tell us, in your voice.
  • Same-day dispatch on orders placed before 2pm. Local hand-delivery in eligible areas; vetted couriers everywhere else. Free local delivery on orders over $80.
  • We never include marketing inserts. The card is the only paper.
  • We follow up 24 hours after delivery — only because we'd want to know if it arrived well.

An invitation.

If a moment calls and you don't know what to send — message us. We'll suggest. We've been at this long enough now that we usually have a quiet idea, and we don't push.
