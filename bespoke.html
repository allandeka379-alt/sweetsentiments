Some sentiments don't fit into a category. A 50th-anniversary bouquet of 100 yellow roses. A surprise hotel-room setup with a hand-bound book in someone's grandmother's handwriting. A jersey-of-choice in a cream box, found and flown in.

The bespoke service exists for the gifts that need to be unrepeatable. Tell us the moment, the recipient, the budget, and the brief. We will sketch a proposal, send a price, and if it lands — we'll source, assemble, and deliver the entire thing for you.

Most bespoke commissions take 7–14 days from brief to delivery. We will need at least one phone call. The minimum spend is $150.
